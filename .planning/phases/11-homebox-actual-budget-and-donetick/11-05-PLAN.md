|----------|-------------|
| tailnet client -> firebat tsnet listener | Untrusted household device traffic terminates TLS at the caddy-tailscale node |
| firebat -> ser8 tcp/2021 | Plaintext HTTP over the LAN, same established pattern as the other three household services |
| circle join/accept flow | A pending join request cannot become active without the circle admin's explicit accept -- the trust boundary between "requested" and "member" |
