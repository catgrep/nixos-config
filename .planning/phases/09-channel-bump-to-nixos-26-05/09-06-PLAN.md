<objective>
Make the smoketest layer capable of failing honestly, and remove the two structural reasons the firebat and ser8 gates cannot currently be trusted.

Purpose: plans 03 and 05 both rest on smoketests as the regression gate for a kernel and channel bump. Right now that gate has three defects that make a green result meaningless. Every suite entry point runs its tests in a bare loop and exits with the status of the last one, so intermediate failures vanish. The NordVPN suite that plan 03 wants to fan out to takes the VPN interface down mid-run with no cleanup trap. And firebat's gateway test iterates a Caddy route whose backend is the physically disconnected pi4, which makes plan 07's firebat gate impossible to pass on the current network.

Output: a shared fan-out helper whose exit status reflects every test it ran, a routine-versus-disruptive split of the NordVPN suite with a trap on the disruptive one, a non-mutating qBittorrent confinement check, the retired AdGuard route deleted from the Caddyfile, one named skip guard governing every pi4 DNS lookup, and the pi4 DNS suite retired.
</objective>
