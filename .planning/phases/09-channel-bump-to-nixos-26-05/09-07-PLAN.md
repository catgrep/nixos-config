## Executor note: firebat is not ZFS

ser8's pre-activation snapshot in plan 09-05 used a ZFS snapshot of its persist dataset. **That mechanism is not available here.** firebat's root filesystem is ext4 and its `impermanence.nix` only pins SSH host keys — there is no ZFS pool, no persist dataset, and no rollback target. Do not look for one, and do not substitute a plain `cp` because the snapshot route is missing. The Grafana database needs SQLite's own online backup, and the resulting file needs somewhere durable to live, which on this fleet means ser8's backup pool.
